### Ref 2 (primary for structure and words): the user's morning-brief screenshot (`refs/morning-brief-reference.webp`)
Take (structure, order, density, word patterns)
- A greeting, large, with a quieter phrase beside it ("Good morning" · the day).
- A routine card: icon tile, name ("Morning Brief"), the schedule in words ("Weekdays · 7:00 AM"), a state (check +
  "Done") and the time.
- Rows of what was read: source logo in a small tile, one past-tense sentence with a real count ("Read today's 3
  meetings"), a check at the right, hairlines between rows, rows about 40px.
- Then the brief: its name again with the day and time ("Wednesday · 7:00 AM"), then three numbered one-line sentences.
- A last quiet line: "Next run tomorrow, 7:00 AM".

Our words in those patterns: "Morning brief", "Every day · 9:00 AM", "Prepared" / "Waiting until 9:00 AM" / "Needs
retry", "Read today's 3 meetings", "Read 4 new emails", "Checked 2 Canvas deadlines", "Went through 6 open
commitments", "Next brief: tomorrow, 9:00 AM".

Leave: its cream colours and serif greeting, the green "Done" pill, sidebar, tab bar, product name, "Weekdays" (TARS
runs every day), and any row not backed by a stored count ("Scanned overnight email" with no number).
