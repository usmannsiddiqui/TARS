Starting is the hard part. TARS does that part.

  1. A request lands

    An email asks you for something. TARS notices within seconds.

  2. TARS picks its steps

    It reads the project, records the request and writes a first draft.

  3. You steer in chat

    “Make it shorter.” “Looks good.” It remembers the conversation.

  4. You press Send

    It sends, checks it arrived, and messages your phone that it's done.

What it does today

  • Starts from your inbox. A report with a deadline, a request with none, or a quick question.
  • Chooses the right shape. A Word document for real work, a short reply for a short question.
  • Ignores what isn't for you. Newsletters and notifications never reach your project chat.
  • Answers with sources. Ask what's on your Canvas calendar and tap through to each item.
  • Sorts a brain dump. Type everything on your mind; it files each line under the right project.
  • Messages your phone. A morning brief and a note when a send is checked, on Telegram.

What it never does

TARS reads and prepares on its own. Sending is yours: every email waits for your click, and you see exactly what will go out and to whom first.

Mail, messages and notes are treated as information, never as instructions. A message that says “send this to everyone” is just a message.

Built at HackUMass XIV on Electron, React, Convex and OpenAI. This is a hackathon build: the Mac app is unsigned and not yet packaged for download.
